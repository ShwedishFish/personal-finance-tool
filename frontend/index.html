Your data lives only in this app's local database. A backup is one JSON file with every account, balance, plan, and transaction: keep copies somewhere safe, or restore it on another computer. Restoring replaces everything here. Bank links are not included; reconnect institutions after restoring elsewhere.

Income & goals

Amounts are per year in today's dollars. Goals (a house, a wedding) are one-time unless you set an end age.

Spending

Use one General spending item, or break spending into categories. Items can repeat every few years (a car every 8 years, a roof every 20).

Homes

Your home now, one you plan to buy, or a downsize: sell one and buy another the same year. Mortgage payments and carrying costs are added to spending; sale proceeds go to savings, with gains above the $250k/$500k home-sale exclusion taxed.

Kids

Yearly cost changes by age, then college. Future children work too: enter the expected birth year.

Social Security

When should you claim Social Security?

Roth conversions

Moving 401k/IRA money to a Roth in low-income years (after retirement, before required withdrawals) can lower lifetime taxes. You pay tax now on what you convert.

Healthcare in retirement

Per person: private insurance from retirement until Medicare at 65, then Medicare premiums, supplemental coverage, and out-of-pocket costs. Low enough income before 65 earns ACA premium tax credits; high incomes add Medicare surcharges (IRMAA), based on income two years earlier.
